IoT · Embedded systems · Smart agriculture

IoT Controlled Hydroponic System

A sensor-enabled hydroponic setup designed around the Nutrient Film Technique (NFT). An ESP32 collects environmental and water-temperature readings, sends data to Arduino IoT Cloud over Wi-Fi, and supports remote monitoring and control of the water pump and grow lights.

01SensorsLight, air temp/humidity, water temp

03Wi-Fi + CloudArduino IoT Cloud dashboard
↔
04ControlRelay, pump and grow light

Hardware and platform

  • ESP32 WROOM-DAMain controller with Wi-Fi connectivity.
  • LDR sensorDetects ambient light intensity.
  • DHT11 sensorMeasures room temperature and humidity.
  • DS18B20 sensorMeasures water temperature.
  • 2-channel relay, pump and grow lightsSwitches the connected actuators.
  • Arduino IoT CloudRemote display and switching interface.

What the system does

  1. Collects sensor readings from the growing environment and nutrient-water loop.
  2. Sends readings from the ESP32 to Arduino IoT Cloud via Wi-Fi for viewing.
  3. Lets a user switch the water pump and grow lights through the cloud interface using a relay module.
  4. Uses ambient-light sensing for grow-light logic; the report describes lights turning on when sunlight is insufficient.
↗

Future scope in the report: adding pH and TDS sensors, plus exploring renewable-energy integration.
